Merchant Ecosystem Solutions (MES)

ICICI Bank has built a complete toolkit called Merchant Ecosystem Solutions (MES) to handle this. Introduction to MES
What is Merchant Ecosystem Solutions and why does it matter?
🏪
Meet the Merchant
Ravi owns two grocery stores — one in Pune, one in Nashik. Customers pay by cash, UPI, and card. He pays his 3 suppliers by cheque every week and gives salaries in cash. He uses a different app for each payment method and still has to visit the bank for some things. He has no way to see how both stores are doing together. This is the exact problem MES was built to solve.
🏦
What exactly is Merchant Ecosystem Solutions (MES)?
▼

MES is ICICI Bank's complete toolkit that helps any business handle all its money needs in one place — receiving payments from customers, making payments to vendors and staff, and connecting their accounting software directly to the bank.

It has three pillars:

  • Collections — Tools to receive money: POS, QR/UPI, Payment Gateway, E-Collection, Door Step Banking
  • Payments — Tools to send money: BizPay360, I-Multipay
  • Connected Banking — Tools to link accounting software to ICICI Bank: API Banking, H2H, Tally Plugin
Think of it like thisMES is like a Swiss Army Knife for a merchant's financial life. Instead of using 5 apps from 5 different companies, ICICI gives everything in one ecosystem — all linked to one current account.
🎯
As an RM, how do I figure out which MES product to offer a merchant?
▼

Always start with three simple questions:

  • How does the merchant currently receive money from customers?
  • How does the merchant currently pay vendors, staff, suppliers?
  • Does the merchant use any accounting software like Tally?

Based on the answers, you map them to the right product. A small retailer needs QR and POS. A school needs Bill Upload. A factory paying 500 vendors needs I-Multipay or H2H.

Golden Rule for RMsNever pitch a product first. Always understand the problem first. Ask → Understand → Recommend.
💡
Why would a merchant prefer ICICI MES over using Paytm, PhonePe, or other apps?
▼

When a merchant uses ICICI MES tools, all money — from QR, POS, online orders, and bank transfers — settles directly into their ICICI Current Account. Benefits:

  • One account, one view — no juggling between multiple apps and banks
  • T+1 settlement — money in account by next working day, predictably
  • Loan eligibility — ICICI can offer business loans based on transaction history
  • Single RM — one point of contact for all banking needs
Real scenarioIf Ravi uses PhonePe QR and HDFC POS, his collections land in 2 different banks. With ICICI MES, everything lands in his one ICICI account — simpler, safer, and easier to grow from.

QR / UPI — Collecting Payments the Smart Way
Covers: Static QR, Dynamic QR, Sound Box, UPI Dashboard, Expression QR, UPI Intent — and who needs what
☕
Meet the Merchant
Meena runs a busy tea stall near a bus stand. 80–100 customers a day. Half ask "UPI hai kya?" She has a PhonePe QR stuck on her counter, but she's always busy making tea and cannot check her phone. She once got cheated by a fake payment screenshot. She has no idea what her total daily collections are. She has no way to know if a payment failed. ICICI Bank's QR solutions solve every single one of these problems.
📱
What is Static QR and what is Dynamic QR — and which merchant needs which?
▼

Static QR is a fixed printed code. The customer scans it and types the amount manually. It never changes — laminate it and stick it on the counter forever.

Dynamic QR is generated fresh for each transaction with the exact bill amount already loaded. Customer just scans and pays — no typing, no error.

Who needs what?Meena's tea stall → Static QR is perfect. Prices are standard (₹10, ₹20). Simple and free.

A restaurant billing ₹1,248 after a full meal → Dynamic QR. The billing software generates the bill, QR auto-loads the exact amount. No chance of a customer entering the wrong number.

Zero transaction chargesSettles to ICICI CAT+1 settlement

🔊
What is the Sound Box (EazySound) and exactly who benefits from it?
▼

The Sound Box is a small speaker device that sits at the merchant's counter and announces payment confirmation out loud the moment a UPI payment arrives — in the merchant's chosen language. It supports 8 languages.

Critically, it also announces failed transactions — so the merchant knows immediately if something went wrong, without checking a phone.

Solves Meena's exact problemMeena is making tea and cannot look at her phone. With the Sound Box she hears "Payment received ₹30" without looking. No more fake screenshot tricks. If a payment fails, she hears that too.

One-time installation: ₹499. Monthly rental: ₹150.

Best for: Busy countersNoisy shopsStreet vendorsMedical storesPetrol pumps

📊
A merchant has multiple outlets. How does ICICI Bank help them track all counters together?
▼

The Merchant QR/UPI Dashboard is a web portal where each outlet or counter is added as a sub-merchant under the main merchant's account. The owner can see all counters in one unified view — daily, weekly, and monthly breakdowns by counter, mode, and amount.

Key features: real-time updates, reports downloadable with payer details, search by RRN number for dispute resolution, and the ability to delegate access to a manager.

Imagine thisA clothing showroom owner in Mumbai has 4 billing counters across 2 floors. From home at 9 PM, she sees Counter 3 collected ₹45,000 today while Counter 1 only did ₹12,000. She knows exactly where to focus tomorrow morning — without having visited the store.
🚚
A merchant collects payment at the customer's doorstep. How does UPI work without the customer scanning a code?
▼

UPI Intent is the answer. The merchant sends a payment request via SMS to the customer's mobile number. The customer receives it on their phone and approves — from wherever they are. No physical QR code needed.

Live use case — Home delivery medicine shopA pharmacy in Bengaluru delivers medicines worth ₹760 to a customer's home. Instead of the delivery person carrying a QR standee or handling cash, the pharmacist sends a UPI payment request to the customer's number. Customer approves from inside their home. Zero cash, zero risk, clean digital trail.
🎨
What is Expression QR and does it actually matter for a merchant's business?
▼

Expression QR is a branded, customized QR code — the merchant chooses a design template that matches their industry (restaurant, pharmacy, clothing, etc.) and gets it printed as a professional standee or sticker.

Functionally it works the same as a Static QR — but it looks designed and professional, not just a paper printout.

Why it matters more than it looksImagine two restaurants. One has a scruffy printed QR taped to the wall. The other has a branded ICICI Bank Expression QR standee with the restaurant's logo. The second one signals: "We are established. We are professional. Trust us." Perception matters in business.

Ordered via: InstaBIZ App → My QR → Expression QR

POS Machine — Collecting Card Payments In-Store
Who needs a POS, what does it do, and how does it compare to QR?
🛍️
Meet the Merchant
Arnav runs an electronics showroom with average bill sizes of ₹8,000–₹50,000. Most customers pay by credit card or debit card. Some want to pay via EMI. A QR code works for UPI, but not for credit card swipes, EMI offers, or brand-specific discounts. He is currently using a competitor's POS machine, paying high rental and getting poor service. This is exactly the merchant profile that needs ICICI Bank's Android POS.
💳
What is a POS machine and what type of merchant actually needs one?
▼

POS stands for Point of Sale — it is the device at a merchant's counter where a customer swipes, dips, or taps their debit or credit card to pay. ICICI Bank's POS is an Android-based smart terminal (APOS) — not a basic card reader, but a full smart device that also accepts Dynamic QR payments.

A merchant needs POS when:

  • Customers pay by credit card or debit card (QR only handles UPI)
  • The merchant wants to offer EMI on credit cards
  • High-ticket purchases where customers prefer card payments
  • The merchant wants a billing system integrated with the payment device
QR vs POS — the key differenceQR only accepts UPI payments. POS accepts Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Dynamic QR, and supports EMI and Brand EMI — making it essential for high-ticket retail.
📱
What makes ICICI Bank's POS (APOS) different from a regular swipe machine?
▼

ICICI's APOS (Android POS) is a 5.5-inch touchscreen Android 11 device — not a simple card reader. It is a smart terminal with:

  • NFC (Tap to Pay) — customer taps phone or card, no swipe needed
  • Dynamic QR integration — accepts UPI alongside card payments
  • Voice notification — confirms payment out loud (like Sound Box)
  • App dashboard — daily and weekly analysis on the device itself
  • Brand EMI and CC EMI — merchant can offer EMI on specific brands
  • Billing system and ERP integration — connects to merchant's billing software
Think of it like thisA regular swipe machine is like a basic phone from 2010 — it only calls. ICICI's APOS is like a smartphone — it handles payments, shows analytics, integrates with billing software, and accepts tap, swipe, dip, and QR all in one device.
🏪
Which type of merchant should an RM pitch POS to? Who should NOT get a POS?
▼

Offer POS to:

  • Mass retail — grocery chains, medical stores, clothing stores
  • Mid-size merchants — restaurants, electronics shops, jewellers
  • Large format stores — supermarkets, malls, hospitals — especially with ERP/billing system

Do NOT offer POS to:

  • Very small street vendors or stalls (QR is sufficient and free)
  • Businesses that are not in the approved merchant category list

Important operational note: Retail/MES ZH approval is required for each POS merchant. The window to add a new POS merchant opens only twice a month per zone per RH — so RMs must plan in advance.

RM tipIf a merchant only receives UPI payments and has low ticket sizes (under ₹500), a QR code is sufficient and free. Save POS for merchants who need card acceptance, EMI, or billing integration.
🃏
What card types and payment modes does the ICICI APOS accept?
▼

Cards accepted: MasterCard ✅ | Visa ✅ | RuPay / Diners Club / JCB / UnionPay ✅ | Prepaid / Gift Card (with PIN) ✅ | Amex ❌

Payment modes: Credit Card ✅ | Debit Card ✅ | Dynamic QR ✅ | NFC (Tap to Pay) ✅

Eazypay Classic — Online & Offline Payment Collection
4 modes for every merchant — with or without a website
🏫
Meet the Merchant
Priya manages a school with 800 students. Every term she has to collect fees from 800 families. She currently does this by sending individual WhatsApp messages asking parents to pay. Some pay by NEFT, some by cheque, some are always late. She has no way to send automated reminders or track who has paid. She does not have a school website. Eazypay Classic solves Priya's problem — and it works for merchants with or without a website.
💡
What is Eazypay Classic and what makes it different from just having a QR code?
▼

Eazypay Classic is ICICI Bank's Payment Gateway product that enables merchants to collect payments through multiple modes — online and offline. Unlike QR/POS which collect money in-person, Eazypay Classic collects money without the customer being physically present.

It supports: Credit Cards, Debit Cards, UPI, Netbanking, NEFT/RTGS, Cash/Cheque, and International Cards.

The key differentiator: it works with OR without a merchant website — making it accessible to schools, NGOs, service providers, and small businesses with no tech setup.

QR vs Eazypay — simply putQR = customer comes to your shop and scans a code.
Eazypay Classic = customer is sitting at home, gets a link or sees your name on the portal, and pays — you never even meet them.
🌐
Mode 1 — Payment Gateway (PG) Mode: Which merchant needs this?
▼

PG Mode is for merchants who have a website or mobile app. ICICI's payment gateway is directly integrated into the website. When a customer shops on the website and reaches the checkout page, they see all payment options — cards, UPI, netbanking, wallets.

Features: iOS and Android integration, 3 plugins (WordPress, Magento), 4 language support, Intelligent Retry (failed payments auto-retried), daily MIS, merchant dashboard.

Who needs PG Mode?An e-commerce clothing brand selling online. A healthcare startup with an app for doctor appointments. Any business with an online storefront where customers shop and pay digitally.

Key feature — Intelligent Retry: If a customer's UPI payment fails mid-transaction, ICICI's PG automatically retries it. This reduces lost sales — a direct revenue benefit for the merchant.
📧
Mode 2 — CIB/Bill Upload: Perfect for which merchant?
▼

CIB/Bill Upload is for merchants who need to bill a large number of customers at once — without a website. The merchant uploads a file (with customer names and amounts) into Corporate Internet Banking. ICICI generates individual payment links and sends them to each customer. Customers click the link and pay at www.eazypay.icicibank.com.

Feature: Automated reminder alerts sent to customers before the payment due date.

Perfect for Priya's schoolPriya uploads one file with all 800 student names and fee amounts. ICICI generates 800 individual payment links and sends them to each parent. Parents get reminders 3 days before due date. Priya gets a real-time dashboard showing who has paid and who hasn't. Zero manual follow-up.

Best for: SchoolsService providersHousing societiesInsurance companies

🔗
Mode 3 — OTC (Over The Counter): When is this the right choice?
▼

OTC is for merchants who deal with customers over the phone or without a website. The merchant generates a payment link on the spot for one specific customer and sends it via SMS or email. Customer clicks and pays.

Special feature: The merchant can restrict payment mode per customer — e.g., only UPI for one customer, only debit card for another, or all modes for everyone.

Who uses OTC?A telemarketing company (like Tele-Deals in the ICICI case study) takes product orders over the phone. For every order, they send a separate payment link with an expiry date. Customer pays at their convenience. Zero physical card needed. Exactly like sending a payment link on WhatsApp — but ICICI's secure, tracked version.

Best for: TelesalesCNP transactionsService-based businessesTravel agents

Mode 4 — Open/Push Mode: Who uses this and why?
▼

Open/Push is for merchants who want one open payment link shared with everyone — where the customer decides the amount they want to pay. There is no fixed invoice or amount.

The NGO use caseHelping Hands NGO shares one donation link on social media: "Click here to donate." Anyone who wants to donate clicks, enters ₹100 or ₹5,000 — whatever they wish — and pays. No individual invoices, no fixed amounts. The NGO's name appears searchable on www.eazypay.icicibank.com so anyone can find and donate.

Best for: NGOsCharity organizationsCrowdfundingReligious trusts

E-Collection — Tracking Who Paid What via NEFT/RTGS
For businesses that receive large bank transfers and struggle to identify which customer paid
🏭
Meet the Merchant
Suresh runs a wholesale distribution business. 80 retailers transfer money to his account every month via NEFT or RTGS. When ₹3 lakh appears in his account, he has no idea which retailer sent it. His accounts team spends 3 days every month calling each retailer to confirm payments. Some payments are mismatched. Reconciliation is a nightmare. E-Collection gives each of Suresh's 80 retailers their own unique account number — and Suresh instantly knows who paid what.
🔢
What is E-Collection and how does the Virtual Account Number system work?
▼

E-Collection is an ICICI Bank solution that gives each payer (vendor, retailer, customer) a unique Virtual Account Number (VAN). When this payer sends NEFT/RTGS/IMPS, they use this VAN instead of the merchant's actual account number.

The VAN is structured as: Client Code + Vendor Code. ICICI Bank's system reads this combination and instantly identifies which payer the money came from — before crediting it to the merchant's main current account.

How it works for SureshSuresh gets a unique VAN for each of his 80 retailers: Retailer 1 gets VAN-001, Retailer 2 gets VAN-002, etc. When Retailer 47 transfers ₹3 lakh, ICICI reads VAN-047 and knows it's from Retailer 47. Suresh's account gets credited AND he gets a customized MIS report showing: Retailer 47 paid ₹3 lakh at 11:34 AM. Zero confusion. Zero calls.
📋
What does the MIS report look like and how does it help the merchant?
▼

ICICI sends a customized MIS report hourly or at end-of-day with all transaction details — payer name, amount, time, and VAN. This report can be directly uploaded into the merchant's accounting software for auto-reconciliation.

Additional features:

  • Option to send payment confirmation to each payer automatically
  • MIS at hierarchy level — branch-wise, region-wise, distributor-wise
  • Real-time ERP update possible via H2H or API integration
Time saved for SureshBefore E-Collection: 3 days per month spent on reconciliation. After E-Collection: MIS auto-generated, uploaded directly into accounting software. Reconciliation done in minutes — not days.
💬
How does an RM identify a merchant who needs E-Collection? What questions to ask?
▼

Ask these probing questions:

  • "Is reconciliation of payments a problem in your business?"
  • "Do you face challenges in identifying which customer sent a bank transfer?"
  • "Do your customers (payers) get timely updates on whether their payment was received?"
  • "Do you find the cost of setting up collection infrastructure high?"

If the merchant answers "yes" to any of these — E-Collection is the right conversation.

Pitch to Suresh"Sir, instead of calling 80 retailers every month to confirm payments, what if each retailer had their own unique payment number and you got a report every hour showing exactly who paid what? That's ICICI E-Collection."

Door Step Banking (DSB)
When the merchant cannot come to the bank — the bank comes to them
🚐
What is Door Step Banking and which merchant would actually use it?
▼

Door Step Banking (GBO DSB) is an ICICI service where a bank-assigned agency visits the merchant's premises to pick up or deliver cash, or to pick up cheques — eliminating the need for the merchant to visit the bank branch.

Two service types: Fixed Beat (agency visits daily at a fixed time — for high-volume daily cash merchants) and On Call (merchant requests as needed).

Who needs this?A large supermarket or petrol pump collecting ₹5–10 lakhs in cash daily. Sending a staff member to the bank every day is risky (cash in transit), expensive (manpower), and time-consuming. With DSB, the bank comes to them — safe, scheduled, and professional. Also useful for businesses that issue a large number of cheques and need them picked up for deposit.

BizPay360 vs I-Multipay — Paying Vendors, Staff and Suppliers
Two ICICI products for outgoing payments — learn which merchant needs which
🚚

Kavitha runs a logistics company with 150 delivery partners and 30 vendors. 💸
What is BizPay360 and which merchant is it perfect for?
▼

BizPay360 is ICICI's digital bulk payment platform. A merchant uploads one file and hundreds of payments — NEFT, RTGS, IMPS — go out simultaneously. No need to initiate each payment one by one.

Key features:

  • Instant digital activation — no paperwork, start immediately
  • Choose debit type — consolidated (one debit for all) or individual (separate debit per payment) — in the same file
  • Macro-based validation — catches errors in the file before payments go out
  • DIY customization — merchant can change file formats, settings themselves
  • MIS download in Excel, Text, or PDF
  • Up to 10 on-screen payments with a single OTP
Kavitha's use case with BizPay360Kavitha uploads one file with 150 delivery partner payments (individual debit — one entry per person) and 30 vendor payments (consolidated debit — one combined entry). Both in the same file. All 180 payments go out together. Her bank statement is clean, traceable, and reconciled automatically. What took 3 days now takes 30 minutes.
🏢
What is I-Multipay and what does it offer that BizPay360 cannot?
▼

I-Multipay is ICICI's enterprise-grade payment solution for businesses with complex payment needs. The key differentiator: I-Multipay supports physical payment instruments — Cheques, Demand Drafts (DD), and Internet Based Cheque Writing (IBCW).

Additional features BizPay360 does NOT have:

  • Single file with more than 2,000 transactions
  • Host-to-Host (H2H) integration — server-to-server automatic file transfer
  • Customized SMS/Email alerts to each payee after payment
  • ERP customization — the system adapts to the merchant's existing ERP format
  • Scheduled future date payments
  • Beneficiary validation before payment is sent
Kavitha's vendor who needs a chequeThree of Kavitha's vendors refuse to accept electronic payments — they want a physical cheque. With I-Multipay, Kavitha can issue unlimited cheques printed at ICICI Bank's location. The vendor gets the cheque — Kavitha does not have to visit a bank to issue it.
🖨️
What are I-Multipay's physical payment products — Cheque, DD, and IBCW?
▼

I-Multipay handles physical payments through three modes:

  • Cheque Printing (RCP_CC) & Demand Draft (DD) — Printed at ICICI Bank's location. Unlimited quantity. Positive Pay validation at clearing for security.
  • IBCW (Internet Based Cheque Writing) — The merchant prints the cheque at their own premises through a web browser. The HO uploads the file; any branch location can print. No delivery time — faster than waiting for bank-printed cheques.
Company with multiple offices — IBCW solves a real problemA company has offices in Mumbai, Delhi, and Hyderabad. They upload one cheque file from Mumbai HQ. Vendors in all three cities get cheques printed locally at each office. No courier, no delay, no centralized queues.
🔍
How does an RM decide between BizPay360 and I-Multipay? What is the decision rule?
▼

The decision is simple — ask the merchant these questions:

  • "Do you need to issue physical cheques or Demand Drafts to any vendors?" → I-Multipay
  • "Do you have more than 2,000 transactions in a single payment file?" → I-Multipay
  • "Do you need your ERP system to connect directly to the bank for payments?" → I-Multipay
  • "Do you want a simple digital bulk payment tool that can be activated today?" → BizPay360
The simple ruleBizPay360 = Digital-first, self-serve, fast to activate. Perfect for most SMEs and mid-size businesses.

I-Multipay = Enterprise-grade, handles physical payments, large volumes, ERP integration. For bigger, more complex businesses.

Settlement & Reconciliation — Key Concepts
Concepts that appear across all MES products — explained once, clearly
📅
What does T+1 Settlement mean and why do merchants care about it?
▼

T = Transaction date. T+1 = Next working day.

When a customer pays a merchant via POS, QR, or Payment Gateway, the money does not appear in the merchant's account instantly — it goes through a settlement cycle. T+1 means the money reaches the merchant's ICICI Current Account by the next working day.

Why merchants care deeply about thisA supermarket collects ₹10 lakh on a Saturday. With T+1, the money is in the account by Monday morning — available for paying suppliers on Monday. If settlement was T+5, the merchant would have a cash crunch for 5 days. T+1 gives merchants working capital predictability.
📋
What is MIS and why is it important for a merchant?
▼

MIS = Management Information System — essentially a detailed report of all transactions. In ICICI MES, almost every product generates a MIS report: POS MIS, Payment Gateway MIS, E-Collection MIS, BizPay360 MIS.

The MIS tells the merchant: who paid, how much, when, through which mode, and the transaction status.

Why MIS mattersWithout MIS: Merchant spends hours manually matching bank statement with their sales records — prone to errors.

With MIS: All transaction data in one structured report — downloadable in Excel, PDF, or text — that can be directly uploaded into accounting software. Reconciliation that used to take 3 days now takes 30 minutes.
✅
What is Reconciliation and why is it a pain point for merchants?
▼

Reconciliation = matching what the merchant's books say vs what the bank statement says.

Every business has to do this — for every payment received and every payment made. If the numbers do not match, there is an accounting error or a missing/extra payment.

Manual reconciliation (without ICICI MES tools) is tedious, error-prone, and time-consuming — especially for businesses with hundreds of daily transactions.

How ICICI MES solves reconciliation across productsE-Collection: MIS report auto-ready for accounting software upload.
BizPay360: File sequence number in debit narration for easy matching.
Tally Plugin: Auto-voucher creation and instant reconciliation within Tally.
H2H: Reverse MIS auto-updates back into ERP after bank processes file.

The common theme: ICICI removes the manual step from reconciliation wherever possible.
